# Research prototype disclaimer

ORBITAL is a software demonstration, not a medical device, clinical monitoring service or diagnostic system. Crew profiles, measurements, alert parameters and recovery examples are fictional. No treatment, risk probability or medical testing schedule is prescribed.

Public ECG research imports contain published numerical features with explicit source/quality limits. The app does not analyze ECG images/PDFs/raw waveforms, connect live sensors or validate actual astronaut health. The anatomy model is reference geometry, not personalized anatomy. NASA resources provide context, not endorsement.

Records stay in local browser storage and may be cleared or evicted. Keep an export for recovery. Offline operation requires an initial installation; software tests do not establish clinical safety or accuracy.
