# ORBITAL — 30-second team demo

Three speakers, about ten seconds each. The complete script is 71 words. Speak naturally, with a short pause at each handover. Names can appear as lower-thirds so introductions do not consume the video.

| Time | Speaker | Exact lines | Show |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–10 s | Person 1 — introduction and purpose | “Hi, we’re the team behind ORBITAL. We connect astronaut health readings to personal baselines, clear evidence and practical follow-up in one traceable workflow.” | Overview, then a short Trends clip |
| 10–20 s | Person 2 — working example | “Here, repeated fictional bone measurements highlight the reported femur. The astronaut is prompted to verify the site and device, and report any discomfort.” | Atlas warning, then the astronaut questions in Overview |
| 20–30 s | Person 3 — safeguards and outcome | “Uncertain readings stay unhighlighted. We track task completion separately from recovery, and clear the finding only when compatible repeat evidence meets our fictional demonstration rule.” | Task completed while warning remains; then cleared highlight |

## Recording setup

1. Open **Overview → Demo scenarios → Choose a demo file → Load bone change demo**. The demo controls sit below the Flight log.
2. Open **Atlas**, inspect the left femur and use **Isolate selected** for a clear close-up.
3. Return to **Overview**, confirm site/device and record the fictional astronaut’s response. Capture the first repeat showing task completion with a recovering warning, then the second showing the cleared highlight.
4. Record these as short clips and edit cuts between them. Preload the atlas before recording; loading time is not part of the 30 seconds.
5. Keep the caption **Research prototype · fictional, time-compressed replay** visible. End card: **ORBITAL — traceable measurements, explainable follow-up**.

## Optional substitutions

Use these *instead of* a sentence if a different clip is stronger; do not append them all:

- Person 1: “Every reading retains its original source, units and measurement context.”
- Person 2: “The same workflow connects numerical reports, personal references and the body map.”
- Person 3: “Missing or low-quality data stays explicit, and the app works offline after installation.”

The defensible contribution is the connected evidence-to-follow-up workflow, explicit uncertainty and separate task/recovery state. Describe it as the team’s integration and design work; no world-first, diagnostic, live-sensor or NASA-validation claim is made.
